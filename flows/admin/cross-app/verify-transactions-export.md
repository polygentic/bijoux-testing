## UAT ID

- Logged in as admin@bijoux.app / Test1234!

## Steps
1. Navigate to http://localhost:3001/transactions
2. Look for an "Export" or "Download CSV" button
3. Click the export button
4. Verify a file downloads
5. Take a screenshot
